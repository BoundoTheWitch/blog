### Let's get personal

Hello! I am a nerd. I enjoy listening to mostly metal, Lo-Fi, and video game soundtracks. Of course I also enjoy playing video games, and I have an interest in programming and coding. ### Technical Know-how

Since 2006 when I was 13-years-old I was curious on how websites were made without programs like Dreamweaver. So over a weekend I learned HTML and CSS. From there I experiemented, studied other website's code, looked up more tutorials; I kept going after learning the basics is what I'm trying to get at. And yes, this was during the days where making a layout using tables was common. Web 2.0 was wild before floats, and flexbox and grids. Since HTML5 and CSS3 came out I've studied on that too, including learning semantic tags and learning how to make a website more accessible.

HTML and CSS was not all that I self-taught myself, I also had a copy of Photoshop 7 thanks to my dad, and I learned how to use it for graphic design also through tutorials I found on the web. While I would not consider myself a graphic designer (or a designer in some cases) I am not shy with opening up a photo editor like Photoshop or GIMP to make something if need be. Be it a logo, or modifying a graphic.

While attending college I took some courses on programming in languages like C#, Java, Python (2.7), C++, and JavaScript. I also took a course on MySQL. While I never got to take advance courses for those languages it did springboard me into getting into advanced JavaScript thanks to learning jQuery, and early versions of ReactJS.
